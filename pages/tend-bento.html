A daily ritual that feels like opening something you like.

Every screen is a board of tiles. Each tile holds one idea, so the app is calm to scan, quick to use, and a little different every time you open it. The phone below is live: tap the steps, start brushing, switch to night.

The app changes mood with the time of day. Scroll inside the phone; try all four tabs.

Why people come back

Built for the 2 minutes you give it, twice a day.

  • Satisfying completionSteps fill, the ring closes, the hero tile settles into "Sleep well". The close of the day feels finished.
  • Something new each nightFinishing the routine unlocks one of 30 short insights. A collection, not a score.
  • Forgiving by designWeeks, partial credit, no streak to break. Missing a night costs nothing.
Design rules

One idea per tile

If a tile needs two sentences of explanation, split it.

3 sizes only

1x1, 2x1, 2x2. Rhythm comes from repeating them.

One hero

One high-contrast tile per screen, the next action.
